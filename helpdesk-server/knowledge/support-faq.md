# Refund Policy

We offer a full refund within 14 days of purchase for any subscription plan.
To request a refund, open a ticket with the subject "Refund request" and include
your invoice number. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method
within 5–7 business days.

Annual plans cancelled after 14 days are not refundable, but you keep access
until the end of the paid period.

# Password Reset

If you cannot log in, click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your
email. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email
does not arrive, check your spam folder or contact support.

# Business Hours

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm (your local time).
The AI assistant is available 24/7 for common questions.

# Two-Factor Authentication

You can enable two-factor authentication (2FA) from Settings → Security. We
support authenticator apps (TOTP) such as Google Authenticator and Authy.
